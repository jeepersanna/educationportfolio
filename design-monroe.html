Work/Design

Web · Trilingual

A school website in three languages

A redesign for my kids’ elementary school, built so families who read English, Spanish, or Chinese all land on the same front door. Butterflies from the school’s own logo, papel picado, and type that works in all three scripts. The page I care most about is the Community Hub: mutual aid, resources, volunteering, and the parent-run chats families actually use, under the line “We take care of each other.” In progress, built for the school to move into its own site.

Role
Design, writing, front-end build
Languages
English, Spanish, Chinese
Status
