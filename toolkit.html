For families/Family meeting toolkit

Family meeting toolkit

Free, printable tools for school meetings where every family can take part, not just the ones who already feel at home.

I designed these for my kids’ multilingual public school, where families speak English, Spanish, and Chinese. They come from what I learned in years of PTA and School Site Council meetings, and from my research on who actually gets a say in school decisions. The short version: participation depends on how a meeting is designed, not on how much families care.

This is a first version. I’m trying these out at my own school this year and will update them as I learn what works.

Use them, change them, and translate them for your own community. If your school speaks other languages, the layouts work for any three.
