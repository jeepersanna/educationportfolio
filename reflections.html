Semester reflections

A short note on each class I’m taking: what I’m learning, and what it’s changing in how I think.

City College of San Francisco

Fall 2026

SOC 21

Introduction to Research Models

This class walks through designing a study from start to finish. My first project is three qualitative interviews; the second is a survey. Writing questions that don’t steer people toward my own answer is harder than it sounds. [Add: your topic, or what surprised you in the interviews.]

SOC 2

Deviance and Crime

Who gets labeled deviant, and who gets to do the labeling? We started with Hills on the “mystification” of deviance, and I annotate each reading against current events and my own life. It connects straight to schools: what happens to a kid once adults decide they’re the “bad” one, and how that feeds the school-to-prison pipeline.

CDEV 67

Child, Family, and Community

We study children inside the systems around them: family, culture, school, and neighborhood. My professor pushes us to drop vague words like “respect” and “environment” and say the specific thing. It’s made my writing sharper everywhere. For my community agency project, I’m interviewing someone at Excelsior Action Group about how neighborhood building supports families and youth.

HIST 12A

US Women’s History to the 1880s

I came in knowing less about this era than I wanted to. The question running through the class is which kinds of power actually moved entrenched systems: protest, investigative reporting, the ballot, boycotts, the courts, or cultural assertion.
