SFECU: a coalition born on a picket line

San Francisco Educators and Communities Unite started during the 2026 teachers’ strike, when parents who had joined educators on the line decided to stay organized. I drafted the mission language and designed the identity and site: a hand-drawn fist, a serif with some fight in it, and colors that read from across a rally. In progress.
