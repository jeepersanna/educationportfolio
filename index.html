Questions I’m working on

Questions I’m working on

  • Who gets a say? Families at my kids’ school are invited to the meetings where budgets and plans get decided. What separates being consulted from having a say?

  • Where do kids get to just exist? Public money pays for programs and supervision. Parents I talk to ask for safe streets and green space. Where does a kid go when no adult has planned anything for them?

  • What does freedom need? I grew up homeschooled, free to follow my interests but less free to question what I was told. That tension still shapes how I think about education.
