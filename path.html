How I got here

Not a straight line. Every stop taught me something about how kids learn and what families need.

  1. Growing up

    Homeschooled

    Through high school, with my mom as my first teacher.

    • Real freedom to follow my interests
    • A clear view of what missing structure can cost
    • I didn’t set foot in a formal classroom until college

  2. 2010 to 2014

    Design school

    BS in Communication Design at the University of Cincinnati, DAAP.

    • Undergraduate teaching assistant in 2010: led critiques, graded, and taught coding and design principles
    • Coursework in user research with surveys and interviews

  3. 2010 to now

    Designer

    Web, print, and campaign design, at agencies and freelance.

    • Clients from P&G brands and Maybelline to Cincinnati Children’s Hospital, startups, and community groups
    • Managed clients, timelines, and many projects at once

  4. 2015

    Parent and play group

    Became a parent. When my daughter was four months old, a group of us families started meeting outside every week.

    • Wild Roots still meets weekly in parks across San Francisco
    • Kids from about 6 months to 12
    • We wrote community standards together, grounded in social emotional learning and social justice
    • When a kid is struggling, we add support instead of pushing them out

  5. 2016 to 2021

    Babywearing educator

    Certified educator and chapter president of Babywearing International of San Francisco.

    • Taught safe babywearing to hundreds of families
    • Led the chapter for five years
    • 50+ hours of facilitation training rooted in social justice and accessibility

  6. 2018

    Forest school

    When our kids were ready for more structure, a group of us parents started a co-op forest preschool.

    • We needed steady hours to work, and traditional preschool in SF was out of reach
    • Families interviewed and hired the teacher
    • We built the curriculum together, and it ran for five years

  7. 2020

    COVID and lockdown

    My oldest was in TK when schools closed, and did most of kindergarten in lockdown.

    • I saw how hard it was for students, and for teachers
    • It made me want community more than ever

  8. 2021

    Community herbalist

    Started working as a community herbalist and health educator.

    • Client consultations
    • Community workshops on plants, seasonal foods, and everyday wellness

  9. 2021

    Public school parent

    Joined the PTA and School Site Council at my kids’ public school.

    • Share school data with families in plain language
    • Make translated materials with the school’s family liaisons
    • Food drives, a coat drive, and a free clothing rack

  10. 2026

    Teachers’ strike

    Organized families during the SFUSD teachers’ strike.

    • Set up childcare rotations so parents could keep working
    • Stood with our teachers on the picket line

  11. 2026

    Back to school

    Started at City College of San Francisco in sociology, child development, and Spanish.

    • Research on who gets a say in school decisions
    • A field study of third places on Ocean Avenue

  12. Next

    Teaching

    Transfer, a master’s in education, and a teaching credential for SF public schools.
